Introduction

Newton is committed to protecting the data of our clients and their customers. This Security Policy outlines the measures we have implemented to safeguard the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of the information entrusted to us.

Scope

This policy applies to all employees and advertisers who access Newton systems. It covers all systems, networks, and applications used to support the service.

Data Protection

Data Classification. Data is classified into three categories — Public, Internal, and Confidential — and handled according to its sensitivity. The personal data we collect is limited to names and email addresses used to identify advertisers.

Data Encryption. Our database is encrypted using AES-256 with deletion protection enabled. File storage is protected using Google Cloud Bucket security. Data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher.

Data Hosting. Our service is built on Google Cloud Platform using a 3-tier architecture with logical segregation of data.

Access Control

User Access Management. Multi-factor authentication is required for access. We apply the principle of least privilege, enforce strong passwords, and enable session management.

Administrative Access. Administrative access is limited to authorized personnel with role-based access controls, and access logs are reviewed regularly.

Network Security

Firewalls are configured to protect our systems, network segmentation is implemented, and intrusion detection and prevention systems are deployed.

Vulnerability Management

We apply patches regularly, with critical updates applied within 24 hours. We also conduct regular security assessments and penetration testing.

Incident Response

We maintain an incident response plan with documented procedures. In the event of a breach, affected customers will be notified within 48 hours.

Compliance and Training

Newton complies with GDPR and CCPA. Our employees receive regular security training.

Third-Party Management

Third-party vendors are assessed and required to adhere to our security policies. Data sharing is limited to Newton's infrastructure.
