Pricing model

Most teams pay for execution — media spend, a platform license, and an agency fee — whether or not the campaign works. Newton's CPCU (Cost-Per-Converted-User) flips that: you pay per user who completes the goal that matters — a purchase, a registration, a deposit — with a zero-platform-fee option. In an expensive US auction, Newton carries more of the risk, and your cost stays tied to outcomes.
